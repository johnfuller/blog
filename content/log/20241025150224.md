I'm excited to see the release of the Essay Architecture book from the work of
Michael Dean. As he says, there are no other works that teach you to write. This
could be an important book to a lot of people. Knowing how to write well will
become more important even as AI may do much of your writing. Writing is a
process of structuring your thoughts. AI can't think for you, and therefore
can't write for you. It must be a collaborative process, or you'll never develop
the ability to do either. Instead, you'll turn into a bot.

- [Essay Architecture](https://www.michaeldean.site/)
